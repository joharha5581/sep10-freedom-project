What is ScreenWriting?

Screenwriting is the process of writing scripts for movies, TV shows, or videos. A screenwriter creates the story, characters, dialogue, and scenes.The script explains what actors say and what happens on screen. Screenwriting uses a special format to organize scenes and actions clearly. It helps directors, actors, and producers turn a story into a film or show.

Modern screenwriting software has transformed the way writers or film producers create and organize scripts for their companies. These software techs format scripts to industry standards, help magnge scenes and characters as well as the plot of a story and track revisions.
